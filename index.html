About me

I'm an Informatics and Computer Science student at Strathmore University, interested in how data can explain patterns, support decisions and become practical tools. I work with Python for analysis and machine learning, and I'm exploring data visualisation with Tableau and Power BI alongside app deployment with Streamlit. I enjoy the space between technical problem-solving and clear communication: understanding the question, examining the evidence, testing an approach and explaining what the results actually mean.
